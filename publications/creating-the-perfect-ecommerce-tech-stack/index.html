Creating the Perfect eCommerce Tech Stack

How to choose, and keep reviewing, the Shopify partners and apps your store depends on.

Barrett Ahern · We Make Websites · Published 15 May 2024 · Updated 5 Nov 2026
This is a summary. The full article is on the We Make Websites blog.

With thousands of Shopify technology partners to choose from, the article argues that merchants should pick partners against their own business needs rather than defaulting to the best-known tools.

It covers building a stack from scratch and updating an existing one, with a worked example from This Works' Shopify store, and says the stack deserves regular review either way.

What the article recommends

  1. Set priorities first. SEO, user experience or operational ease will decide which partners fit.
  2. Match tool complexity to the catalogue. Large catalogues with many variants need more capable search tools than small, simple ranges.
  3. Review as you grow. Partners ship new features and your needs change.
  4. Check the set-up. An app that is badly implemented, or not passing on useful customer data, can cause serious problems.
  5. Look for overlap. Shopify's built-in features may replace a tool carried over from another platform.
  6. Weigh consolidation. One multi-function partner simplifies management, but each function still needs to be strong and the integrations checked.

Who it is for

Merchants building a new Shopify store or tidying an existing stack.
